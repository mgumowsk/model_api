Section 1. Qualify the Audience / Open Loop

Description:
This is the critical opening of your ad. You have only a few seconds to grab attention and qualify your audience. Use an opening sentence that speaks to your audience's pain points and desires while introducing an element of curiosity/the HOOK. The goal is to create an open loop that compels them to keep watching and prevent them from clicking away. The goal is to get them to say, "This is for me." An example of a simple way to implement this is This odd/new/breakthrough/weird HOOK gives BIG BENEFIT there is many similar ways that work really well.

Key Strategy:
- Use direct, specific language that speaks to the exact audience you’re targeting. 
- Build curiosity by suggesting you have a solution they’ve never tried before, making it impossible to ignore.

*Examples:*
- "This incredible DIY method unlocks almost cost free electricity for life"
- "This may seem strange, but if you’re trying to lose weight, stop doing cardio right now. And watch this!"

- "I'm shocked that more people don't know about this strange energy device. It generates an endless supply of clean power simply from the ground beneath your feet.

Section 2. What’s In It for Them?

Description:
Here, you need to quickly answer the viewer's core question: "Why should I care?". Clearly tell them what benefits they can get by continuing to watch. You can introduce the product's mechanism or HOOK that directly addresses their needs and is better than anything they have tried before. You can also dispel common beliefs. Highlight how this will benefit them and others are benefiting from it and entice them with the potential results they can achieve.

Key Strategy:
- Focus on the transformation or outcome or problems they will solve.
- This is where you start positioning the solution as the answer to their problem.

*Examples:*

Description:

Ad Script formula writing guidelines:

- You’re not selling a product, your goal is simply to get the audience to click through to view the product sales page.
- Speak from a position of authority — you’ve heard about something they don’t know, and it might help with their problem.
- Make it sound easy. Don’t imply they will need to purchase anything.
- The first 5-30 seconds are critical. The opening scenes are important for capturing attention.
- Ensure each section flows naturally into the next for a cohesive narrative.
- The script is designed to be suggestive and non-factual, adhering to Google’s policies. Avoid being pushy or clickbaity, but remain intriguing.
- Avoid specific numbers, timeframes, or unverifiable "Proven" "scientific discoveries" to ensure compliance.
- Create curiosity around the mechanism and HOOK to drive clicks.
